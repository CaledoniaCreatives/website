About

We build advertising with AI. Not the other way around.

Caledonia Creatives exists for one reason: high-ticket service businesses don't get the same quality of advertising as consumer brands, because the traditional production model doesn't make sense at their scale. We built an agency around AI production specifically to close that gap.

We don't spread across every industry. We specialize in businesses selling considered, high-value services — the kind where one right customer is worth more than a hundred wrong ones, and the advertising has to reflect that. A generic ad optimized for clicks doesn't work on a buyer weighing a five- or six-figure decision.

That specialization is what AI production actually buys you here: not cheaper ads, but the ability to produce, test, and refine advertising concepts at a pace and cost that a traditional shoot-based model can't match — without lowering the bar on what the work looks like.

Where most of the work is, right now

In practice, that's high-ticket home improvement — pools, saunas, home lifts, basement conversions. Same long sales cycle, same big price tag, same need for advertising that doesn't look like a generic small-business template. For a business in that space, the offer is usually:

  • Their current underperforming ad, remade with better production — same angle and hook, so it's not a guess, it's a proven starting point executed properly.
  • Ad creative made with AI production instead of a traditional camera crew and editor — faster to produce, and cheap enough to make multiple versions of, which matters when testing creative without a big production budget each time.
  • Content built specifically for long-sales-cycle, high-ticket buyers, not adapted from a generic small-business ad template.

Alongside the creative itself, that includes strategy and helping build the brand's voice — not just production.

How we work

We don't have case studies to show yet — we're early, and we'd rather say that plainly than pretend otherwise. What we can tell you honestly is how the work actually gets made:

  • A call to understand the business, the offer, and what's already been tried.
  • Competitor and market research, so the concept is built on what's actually happening — not a guess.
  • Multiple creative directions produced with AI, not one ad — so there's something to actually test.
  • A review and refinement pass before anything reaches you, so the bar stays high even though it's AI-produced.
  • Delivery in the formats you need to run it — ready to go live.

Turnaround is faster than a traditional shoot, because there's no camera crew or edit suite to schedule around — but we're not going to put a number on that we can't actually guarantee yet.

How this started

Ryan and Harley are friends turned business partners. We taught ourselves marketing running our own companies, and decided to put that knowledge to work for other businesses — which is how Caledonia Creatives started.
